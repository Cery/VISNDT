### 3.2 各 Section 详情

#### HeroSection
- 表达：Industrial Inspection Solutions Platform
- 标题：Explore Industrial Endoscope Products and Technical Solutions
- CTA：Browse Products → /products | View Categories → /categories
- 风格：深色工业风 (slate-900)，网格叠加背景
- ❌ 无：Buy Now / Shop / Cart / Price

#### CategorySection
- 展示 4 个冻结产品分类（非 Supplier 分类）：
  - Electronic Video Endoscope
  - Optical Endoscope
  - Fiber Optic Endoscope
  - Pipeline Inspection Camera
- 每个分类卡片链接到 `/products?category={slug}`

#### FeaturedProductsSection
- 4 个 mock 产品数据，无 API 调用
- 使用 ProductCard 组件渲染
- "View All Products →" 链接到 /products
- 字段：name, category, model
- ❌ 无：price / stock / cart / seller

#### SolutionsSection
- 4 个工业应用场景（非供应商方案）：
  - Aerospace Inspection
  - Automotive Inspection
  - Pipeline Inspection
  - Manufacturing Quality Control
- 每个方案有图标 + 标题 + 描述

#### InquiryCTA
- 标题：Need a Custom Inspection Solution?
- 按钮：Request Quotation → /login | Quick Inquiry → /register
- 底部提示：Inquiry 表单将在未来更新中提供
- 不调用 POST /inquiries API（Batch3 实现）
